Rose-Hulman student organization

Rose Aerial Systems

We are a Rose-Hulman student team that designs, builds, and flies unmanned aircraft. Our SUAS group is preparing MeadowHawk for the 2026 competition.

Our purpose

We learn by building and flying

Students work on the parts of an aircraft that match their interests, from aerodynamics and manufacturing to electronics, controls, software, and flight operations. MeadowHawk gives those groups one shared system to solve.

We document our work, test in small steps, and pass what we learn to the next team. That matters just as much as getting the aircraft into the air.

Organization structure

  • Rose Aerial Systems executive committee
  • Student Unmanned Aerial Systems competition program
  • Design Build Fly program
  • Technical and operational subteams
  • Faculty and staff advisors
How we work

Work that crosses subteams

The exact roster changes, but MeadowHawk work generally falls into these four areas. A flight test usually needs people from all four.

Aircraft and structures

Airframe design, CAD, manufacturing, propulsion installation, and mechanical repairs.

Avionics and controls

Power, flight controls, navigation sensors, pilot links, telemetry, and the ground station.

Autonomy and perception

Mission software, simulation, camera integration, detection, mapping, and target location.

Payload and operations

Payload hardware, procedures, safety, field testing, logistics, and competition preparation.
