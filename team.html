Rose-Hulman student organization

Rose Aerial Systems

RAS provides an environment for student collaboration, research and development, practical aerospace experience, and competition-driven systems engineering in unmanned and autonomous flight.

Our purpose

Learning by building systems that must work in the real world

The organization brings together students interested in aerospace, mechanical design, electronics, controls, software, computer vision, manufacturing, testing, safety, and operations. The SUAS program is one of the organization's competition efforts and is led by a dedicated competition lead and subteam structure.

Our work emphasizes technical documentation, safe shop and flight practices, knowledge transfer, and progressive testing so that each year's team can build on the last.

Organization structure

  • Rose Aerial Systems executive committee
  • Student Unmanned Aerial Systems competition program
  • Design Build Fly program
  • Technical and operational subteams
  • Faculty and staff advisors
